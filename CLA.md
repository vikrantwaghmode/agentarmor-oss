## How to sign

We use **CLA Assistant Lite** to collect signatures automatically.

When you open your first pull request, a bot will comment asking you to sign.
To sign, **add a normal comment to that pull request** containing exactly:

```
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your GitHub username, the signing comment, and a timestamp are recorded in
`signatures/version1/cla.json` in this repository as the record of your
agreement. You only need to sign once; future pull requests are recognized
automatically. If a recheck is ever needed, comment `recheck`.
